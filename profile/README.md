# Rouben Malian Memorial Foundation

**Honoring a legacy. Empowering the future.**

The Rouben Malian Memorial Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit built on a simple belief: potential is universal, but
opportunity is not. From New York to Armenia to Sydney, we work to close the gap between the two by tackling the digital
divide, advocating for educational funding, and helping people build the skills and stability to thrive.

Our work follows the "big bet" spirit of our namesake, Rouben Malian: invest in human potential, and hold every part of
the work to the same standard of integrity he held himself to.

## What we focus on

| | |
|---|---|
| **Community Engagement & Education** | Laptops, tablets and hotspots for students who lack access at home, supervised Learning Hubs with high-speed internet, and connecting families with schools and resources |
| **Vocational Training & Career Success** | Digital-literacy courses, vocational workshops in high-demand trades, and career pathways that lead to lasting employment |
| **Health & Family Wellness** | Crisis assistance for families facing sudden hardship or displacement, and cultural and community programs that strengthen belonging |
